A pending entry order now trails instead of cancelling as price retraces, moving with price using Entry_Trail_Brick_Number below (same gap logic as the Stop Loss trail).
This applies in every trade mode. In Long & Short mode only, once price retraces a full brick past the breakout's origin brick, the trailing entry is instead cancelled and an opposite-direction entry is placed. Stop Loss & Entry Trailing

SL_Trail_Brick_Number controls SL distance from price while a position is open (SL = price ± SL_Trail_Brick_Number × brick size).
Entry_Trail_Brick_Number controls how far the pending entry order trails behind price on retracement, before any position is open.
Limit_Offset is shared by both the Stop Loss and the trailing entry's SL-LMT orders.
